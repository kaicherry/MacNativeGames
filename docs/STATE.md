### S2.22 — Keep the audio alive through an interruption (2026-10-23, out of band)

- **The gap.** S2.21's `device_monitor` restarts the queue only when `kAudioQueueProperty_IsRunning` goes false. A system alert once cut the stream with a pop while the flag **stayed true**, so the monitor could not see it, and the guest — which is paced 1:1 by released buffers — then waited on buffers that never came back.
- **The detector is the samples, not the flag.** With `state == STATE_STARTED` and a non-empty queue, a monitor tick that finds `played_samples` unchanged counts toward a stall; `STALL_TICKS` = 3 such ticks (3 × 200 ms) means the queue is wedged, and it is rebuilt. Sizing from `stereo.log`: buffers are ~256 frames (~5.3 ms), `queued` is steady at 2, `appends` ≈ `played`, and a healthy 200 ms period advances `played` by ~9600 frames (~97792 per 2 s trace line) while a stalled one advances none — so 600 ms of zero progress with data queued cannot be healthy, and 600 ms is far short of the 32-deep `queue full` fail.
- **The seed's premise was not used.** The brief said to detect "`appends` advancing while `played` does not"; the trace shows a ~2-deep pool paced by released buffers, so a stall **also** stops appends, and an `appends`-keyed detector would miss the case it exists for. The detector keys on `played` with the queue non-empty.
- **The rebuild.** `rebuild_queue` (called by `device_monitor`) releases the queued tags into `released` via `release_head` (the guest is not left waiting, and `played_samples` stays in step), builds a fresh queue on the current default device's UID, starts it, disposes the old one and signals the buffer event. It logs `the output is fed but playing nothing (interruption); rebuilt on <dev> uid <uid>, N tag(s) returned`.
- **The lock.** A dedicated `aq_lock` guards the queue's lifetime: `append` (allocate + enqueue), the rebuild, `set_state`, `Flush`, `open_device`, `close` and the device-follow block take it, so an append can never enqueue on a queue being disposed. `played` takes `aq_lock` before `o->lock` and frees its buffer only while its queue is still `o->aq`; the rebuild drops the old queue with **no** lock held, so a callback in flight cannot deadlock the dispose and cannot free a freed buffer.

### S2.23 — Give the audio a host-side cushion so frame hitches stop starving it (2026-10-23, out of band)
